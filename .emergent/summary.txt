<product_requirements>
The LuxePass app aims to provide a premium mobile experience with Client, Admin, and Gym interfaces, secured by JWT. Initial requirements focused on fixing critical issues like gym login failures, API inconsistencies, and frontend navigation, while prioritizing payment systems, notifications, and UX.
Later, the scope expanded to include:
-   **Client Features:** Daily supplementation logs, nutrition plans, workout generation, gym check-ins with location/notifications, professional guidance (messaging, scheduling with availability).
-   **Admin Features:** Dedicated Nutritionist/Personal Trainer tabs (manage clients, schedules, plans), gym activation/deactivation, real-time Dashboard. Robust professional login, test premium client, interactive plan creation, patient flagging, professional navigation.
-   **Integrated Scheduling:** Comprehensive system with professional profiles, seamless client assignment, 1-2 monthly consultations based on plan (Basic: gym tokens only; Intermediário: 1 Nutri, 1 Personal; Avançado/VIP: 2 Nutri, 2 Personal), appointment cancellation.
-   **Client Upgrade Paths:** Workout and Nutrition tabs showing plan info, full price, next month validity.
-   **Gym System Enhancements:** Client full name, documents, photo on token validation; client check-in history; revenue reports for gyms (configurable value per check-in); contract management with upload/view options; plan-based access for clients to specific gym types.
</product_requirements>
