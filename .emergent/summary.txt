<product_requirements>
The primary requirement is to integrate a full-featured, automated payment and subscription system into the LuxePass application using the Pagar.me gateway.

**Core Features:**
1.  **Subscription Plans:** Offer three distinct plans (Básico, Intermediário, VIP) with different pricing and an initial admission fee (except for VIP).
2.  **Payment Methods:** Support initial and recurring payments via both PIX and Credit Card.
3.  **Automated Billing:** Credit card payments must be charged automatically each month. For PIX, a new QR code must be generated and the user notified monthly.
4.  **Contractual Agreement:** Before paying, users must review and accept a detailed, legally robust contract. This contract is dynamically populated with user data (Name, CPF), plan details, values, and company information (CNPJ: 60.357.323/0001-69). It must include clauses for a 12-month fidelity period, fines for early cancellation, and a warning about potential credit score impact (negativação) for non-payment.
5.  **Cancellation Policy:** The VIP plan can be canceled anytime without a fee. Other plans, if canceled within the 12-month fidelity period, will incur a fine equal to the sum of the remaining monthly payments.
6.  **Admin Panel:** An admin interface must show all clients, their chosen plan, payment method, payment status (Active, Pending, Blocked), and contract dates. It must allow for manual Cobrar Agora actions.
7.  **Access Control:** After three failed payment attempts, the user's access to key features (like gym token generation and appointments) must be automatically blocked.
</product_requirements>
