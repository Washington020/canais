<product_requirements>
The project, LuxePass, is a comprehensive fitness platform with four interfaces:

1.  **Client App:** Users register, choose a subscription plan (Básico, Intermediário, VIP), and pay via PIX or credit card. They can schedule video consultations with professionals, view their plans, and use tokens for gym access.
2.  **Professional App (Nutritionist/Personal Trainer):** Professionals manage their availability, view their schedule, conduct video consultations, and confirm attendance after a session is completed.
3.  **Admin Panel:** Admins manage gyms and professionals, approve new client registrations after payment, and view a comprehensive dashboard with key metrics like pending revenue, new user counts, and confirmed appointments for payroll purposes.
4.  **Gym App:** Gyms validate client tokens for check-ins.

The core requirement is a fully integrated, real-world system where all parts communicate seamlessly. This includes a complete user lifecycle: registration -> payment -> admin approval -> service usage (consultations/gym access) -> professional attendance confirmation -> admin payroll processing. The video call feature must be a real-time, live interaction.
</product_requirements>
