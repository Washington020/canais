<product_requirements>
The project is a comprehensive fitness and wellness platform, LuxePass, with distinct interfaces for Clients, Gyms, Professionals (Nutritionists, Personal Trainers), and a central Admin panel.

**Core User Requirements:**
1.  **Client App:** Users must be able to register, select a subscription plan (Básico, Intermediário, VIP) with varying prices and activation fees, and pay via PIX (QR Code) or credit card. Once subscribed, they can schedule appointments with professionals (based on plan limits), view workout/nutrition plans, and use tokens for gym check-ins.
2.  **Gym App:** Gym partners need to log in to a dedicated portal to validate client tokens. Upon validation, the gym must see the client's full details (name, photo, documents). The system should also support check-in history and revenue reports.
3.  **Professional App (Nutritionist/Personal Trainer):** Professionals must be able to log in, manage their assigned clients, set their availability on a calendar, create workout/diet plans, and view their schedule of booked appointments. The calendar interface must be interactive, allowing them to easily open and view dates.
4.  **Admin Panel:** Administrators require tools to manage gyms (registration, credential management, password reset), professionals (creation, password reset), and view application-wide data.

The overarching goal is a seamless, real-world operational application where all interfaces are interconnected and function reliably.
</product_requirements>
