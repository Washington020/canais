<product_requirements>
The project is LuxePass, a multi-interface fitness and wellness platform.

**Core User Flows:**
1.  **Client-Side:** Clients can register, select a service (e.g., Consulta Nutricional, Personal Training), view available time slots on a calendar, and book an appointment. These appointments initially have a pending status. Once a professional accepts, the client's agenda updates with the professional's name. Clients can then view diet plans created by their nutritionist and workout plans from their personal trainer. The workout plan is interactive, featuring a session tracker with timers for exercises and rest periods, and a progress bar.

2.  **Professional-Side (LuxeCoach):** Nutritionists and Personal Trainers log in to their respective interfaces. They can set their weekly availability. A Novos Clientes tab displays a queue of pending appointments relevant to their specialty (e.g., nutritionists see Consulta Nutricional requests). They can Assumir Cliente (Accept Client), which links the appointment to them. Once a client is assumed, they can create a corresponding diet or workout plan for that client. Their main Agenda should only show appointments they have personally accepted.
</product_requirements>

<key_technical_concepts>
- **Frameworks**: FastAPI (Python) for backend, Expo (React Native) for frontend.
- **Database**: MongoDB, with significant debugging related to the  async driver's caching behavior.
- **Authentication**: JWT-based for both clients and professionals.
- **Routing**: Expo Router (file-based).
- **Core Logic**: Dynamic generation of available appointment slots based on professionals' weekly schedules. A pending appointments queue system for new client acquisition. Role-based access and data filtering (e.g., nutritionists only see nutrition appointments).
</key_technical_concepts>
